Every team starts the season with a rating carried over from last year (regressed toward the average for its class — a good or bad 2025 doesn't fully follow a team into 2026). From there, every game moves the rating based on who was favored and by how much they won or lost. Beating a team you were supposed to lose to gains a lot; barely beating a team you were expected to blow out gains almost nothing. A decisive win moves the needle more than a nail-biter — but only up to a point, since blowout margins get capped once they stop telling us anything new.
